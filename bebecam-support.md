# BebeCam Support Page

## BebeCam - Baby Photos, No HDR

Welcome to the support center for **BebeCam**.

BebeCam helps you capture your baby's soft skin and warm indoor light naturally, with HDR, AI processing, and Deep Fusion fully turned off.

Use it for natural, unprocessed baby photos.
Use it one-handed while holding your baby.
Use it to keep every shot organized in the BebeCam album.
