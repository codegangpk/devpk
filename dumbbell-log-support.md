# Dumbbell Log Support

## 덤벨로그 - Plan, Track, and Complete Your Workouts

Dumbbell Log is a workout planner and tracker designed to keep every training session focused. Build workout plans, record sets, weights, and reps, use rest timers, and review your progress in one place.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I sync my workout data?

Dumbbell Log uses your private iCloud database to sync workout records and pose photos across devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Make sure you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud Drive is enabled.

### Why do I need notification permission?

Notifications are used only to let you know when a rest timer finishes. You can change this permission at any time in iOS Settings.

### Why do I need camera or photo access?

Camera and photo access are optional and are used only when you add a pose photo to an exercise. You can continue using workout planning and tracking without them.

### What should I try if syncing is unavailable?

Check your network connection, iCloud sign-in status, and available iCloud storage. Then reopen Dumbbell Log and allow a moment for syncing to complete.
